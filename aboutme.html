HOME ABOUT ME

Hi there! My name is Rudra, and welcome to my learning journal.

Growing up, I got along well with computers. Everything came organically, and as I got more opportunities to learn about computers, my appreciation for the technology behind them also increased. This eventually brought me onto the path of becoming a full-stack developer.

Lets connect -

How it all started

It all started during the unfortunate pandemic, accompanied by months-long lockdowns. With nowhere to go outside, I was left at home with nothing but my laptop and a lot of free time.

Curious about computers, I taught myself the fundamentals of programming, and my language of choice was Python. I found it simple and intuitive from the get-go, and by the time restrictions were lifted and school classes resumed, I had become pretty well-versed in the hows and whys of programming.

From there, one thing led to another, and I found myself learning about a variety of fields, including Docker images, Dockerfiles, CI/CD, orchestration, and scaling, followed by more mainstream topics for a fresher like me, including but not limited to the trio of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Things I am looking forward to

With AI dramatically and abruptly shaking up the entire tech industry, a shadow of doubt has befallen us all. But I can't contain my curiosity about what the future has in store for us. With learning anything, including programming, becoming more accessible than ever, the pace of the industry as a whole is something very few, if any, of us have ever witnessed.

With design, development, and deployment happening at breakneck speed, I eagerly and patiently await the newborn post-AI industry that we will all be part of.

Goals and standards

Even with AI changing the paradigm and transforming the very core of how we have thought about programming for decades, I still believe there is great value in learning how computers work and how they communicate behind the scenes to provide billions of users with a seamless, near-magical experience.

So, following my ideology, my immediate goals remain the same as they were years ago: learning as much as I can about computers, then implementing, practicing, and sharing what I learn in the real world.
